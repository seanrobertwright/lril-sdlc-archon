# Notice

This pack encodes the engineering process of **Matt Pocock's skills** (https://github.com/mattpocock/skills, v1.3) as Archon workflows. The command prompts adapt and quote process text from those skills: the grilling format, the `/to-spec` template, the `/to-tickets` vertical-slice rules, the `/triage` roles, agent-brief and out-of-scope formats, the `/code-review` smell baseline, the `diagnosing-bugs` phases, the `/improve-codebase-architecture` report format, the `/retro` categories, and the `/wayfinder` map and ticket types. The files in `setup/templates/` are his seed templates, copied verbatim.

The skills themselves are not redistributed. Workflow nodes load them at run time from the `mattpocock-skills` Claude plugin.
